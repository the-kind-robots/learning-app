### Live retention dots in words list

## Goal
Retention level dots in the words list update live.

## Behavior
- Periodically refresh (e.g., every second) to show decay.
- No manual reload required.

## Acceptance
- Word list renders current retention levels without user action.
- Update interval is stable and lightweight.
