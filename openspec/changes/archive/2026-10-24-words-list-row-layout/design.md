## Source

The mockup is the design canvas «Список слов», artboards `RightPhone.dc.html` (phone) and `RightDesktop.dc.html` (computer). Only the row changes; the shell bar, the search bar, the footer and the 600 px column are the app's as they are.

## Row measures, from the mockup

| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| Row | min-height 56 px, padding 12 px 16 px, gap 16 px, items centred |
| German | 17 px, weight 700, line-height 1.3, black-text, hyphenated, may break anywhere |
| Translations | 15 px, weight 600, line-height 1.35, rgb(107 107 107), 2 px under the German |
| Dot | 12 px circle, the retention colour as now |
| Hover (pointer devices) | background polar, radius 12 px |

The translation grey is darker than `--color-wolf`: wolf on white is 4.48:1, under the 4.5:1 text minimum.
