## Why

The tile hint was reachable only after a wrong answer, and a bank tile could only be tapped. The hint is useful from the first showing, and tiles should be placeable by drag and by typing the start of a word (#605).

## What Changes

- The light bulb is offered on every showing of an example, the first included.
- A bank tile can be dragged straight into the tray.
- With a physical keyboard, typing the start of a word moves its bank tile to the tray.

What each of these must do is stated in the delta spec, not here.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `lesson`: the tile hint is offered from an example's first showing; tiles are placed by tap, drag or typing.
